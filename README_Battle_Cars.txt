Battle Cars - Quest 3 Game README

Game file:
Battle Cars.apk

Platform:
Meta Quest 3 / Android VR

Overview:
Battle Cars is a VR arena combat game built in Unity. The player controls a battle car inside an arena and fights through several enemy waves. Before the fight starts, the player can choose a car type. After waves, the player may choose or upgrade the top weapon. The goal is to survive all waves and destroy every enemy car.

Main Features:
- VR car combat on Meta Quest 3.
- Wave-based enemy spawning.
- Multiple player car choices with different traits.
- Weapon choices between waves, including gun, shotgun, and flamethrower style weapons.
- Health pickup spawning between waves.
- Enemy cars with different attack styles.

How to Play:
1. Install Battle Cars.apk on Quest 3.
2. Open it from App Library > Unknown Sources.
3. At the car selection screen, choose a car.
4. Defeat all enemies in each wave.
5. Between waves, choose a weapon when prompted.
6. Survive until all waves are completed.

Quest 3 Controller Controls:

Left Controller:
- Left thumbstick: Move the car.
  Push the stick in the direction you want the car to move.
- Left trigger: Brake / stop movement.
- X button: Reload for weapons that support manual reload.
- Y button: Self-right / flip recovery if the car is overturned.

Right Controller:
- Right thumbstick: Aim the top weapon.
- Right trigger: Fire weapon.
- Right thumbstick left/right on selection screens: Move between car or weapon options.
- Right trigger on selection screens: Confirm selected car or weapon.
- B button: Drop a mine, if the equipped car/weapon setup includes mines.

In-Game UI:
- Wave text shows the current wave.
- Countdown text shows time before the next wave.
- Health UI shows player health.
- Weapon bar / reload UI may appear depending on the equipped weapon.

Notes:
- If the app does not appear in the normal Quest library, check App Library > Unknown Sources.
- The game is designed to run offline after installation.
- Use the Quest system button to quit the app: press the Meta/Oculus button, then choose Quit.
